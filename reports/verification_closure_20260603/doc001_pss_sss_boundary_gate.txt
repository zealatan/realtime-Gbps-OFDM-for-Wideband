TB Fix Note
  Previous run failed T2 due to AXI-Stream sampling race: the output_collector
  was reading m_axis_tvalid at posedge+1ns (post-NBA), by which point the
  m_fire non-blocking assignment had already cleared hold_valid to 0.
  Fix: removed the #1 delay after @(posedge aclk) in output_collector.
  Reading at the posedge itself (pre-NBA) correctly observes hold_valid=1
  while the DUT simultaneously fires m_fire on that same clock edge.
  This is the standard AXI-Stream sampling point and matches RTL behavior.
  T1 is unaffected (verified: still 17/17 PASS).
