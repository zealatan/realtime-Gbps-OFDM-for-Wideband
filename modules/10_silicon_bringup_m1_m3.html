The PS builds a frame in DDR, MM2S DMA streams it into the transceiver, the loopback carries it through the full RX chain, and the demodulated 4-bit nibbles land back in DDR through the S2MM DMA at the PHY sample rate. An on-chip rx_ber_checker (2652-nibble golden anchor) provides scalable long-run BER; the firmware also re-derives per-frame BER from the DDR capture. Control is packed into the shared ber_ctrl GPIO ([31:30] mode, [29] start, [28] continuous re-emit, [27] MM2S payload).

4 M1 — PHY-Rate S2MM Sink

run_s2mm_test() runs the continuous live-TX loopback with dut_capture in CIRCULAR mode (so the broadcaster's second leg never back-pressures), arms the S2MM DMA to write NF×2652×4 bytes to DDR, then verifies the captured nibbles demodulate to the sent frame.

  • Replaces the old per-frame dut_capture BRAM + JTAG mrd readback with a real PHY-rate hardware sink.
  • NF = 4 frames → all nibbles match golden → BER = 0.
  • Establishes the plumbing every later milestone reuses (S2MM arm → RS → dest → LENGTH).

5 M2-full — Multi-Frame Streaming, Pixel-Perfect

M2-full proves the content survives multi-frame streaming, not just one golden slot:

  • Ping-pong loopback with a seamless payload swap — the TX source can change frames without dropping the stream (sim-proven, silicon datapath BER=0).
  • Arbitrary payload driven from the MM2S DMA bank (a real color-video clip), demodulated and compared frame-by-frame.
  • 48 / 48 frames pixel-perfect on silicon — every pixel of every frame reconstructed exactly.
  • Throughput lifted from 0.64 fps to 96 fps (150×) by removing per-frame firmware overhead, while the 48/48 pixel-perfect result held.
This is the qualitative headline: full-motion imagery crosses the OFDM link and comes out bit-for-bit identical.

6 M3 — True PHY Rate Under Continuous Streaming

6.1   Sustained goodput — 412 Mbps

run_xcvr_throughput() holds the loopback in continuous re-emit and samples two on-chip counters over a fixed 100 ms window:

  • TLAST_COUNT → frames/s → payload goodput = frames × 10608 bits / window.
  • CYC_TOTAL → effective sample rate (samples/frame = 4160).

Measured sustained payload goodput = 412 Mbps — the steady-state PHY rate the receiver holds indefinitely with the FIFO in equilibrium.

6.2   Hardware-paced burst — 11 frames @ 870 Mbps, BER=0

run_m3_continuous() brings the loopback up once and lets the free-running demod stream NF=11 frames into DDR in a single S2MM transfer (HAS_TLAST=0, LENGTH-based — no per-frame firmware in the loop). The S2MM drains the FIFO continuously, so the demod never stalls and the RX never loses lock. It times the whole burst to a completion sentinel and re-derives BER frame-by-frame with a ±192-nibble alignment search.

  • 11 / 11 frames clean, BER = 0 across the burst.
  • Burst rate = 11 × 10608 bits / wall-clock → 870 Mbps.
  • errflags clear (bit3 = STALL_TIMEOUT not set); in_cnt / out_cnt / tlast counters consistent.
Why two numbers (412 vs 870 Mbps). They measure different things and are both real. 412 Mbps is the sustained goodput over a 100 ms window at FIFO equilibrium (§6.1). 870 Mbps is the burst rate while the S2MM drains a pre-filled FIFO faster than steady state (§6.2). The burst is deliberately bounded to NF=11 frames — inside the s2mm_fifo (32768-deep, ≈12 frames) clean window, so no back-pressure occurs yet.

8 Scope & Honest Caveats

  • Datapath, not the P5 sync wrapper. These results are on rx_full_board_top (full-chain loopback). They do not retire the open multi-frame findings against phase3_sync_axis_dma_wrapper in reports/rx_multiframe_streaming_signoff.md (per-slot int-CFO phase-origin and multi-slot re-arm stall). Those are a different integration path.
  • No back-pressure exercised yet. M3a stays inside the FIFO clean window (11 < ≈12 frames). Sustained streaming past the FIFO (with real back-pressure) is the next thing to prove; 412 Mbps (§6.1) is the current sustained figure.
  • Same payload every frame. BER is measured against one golden frame looped (M1/M3) or a known video clip (M2-full) — not random per-frame data.
  • Digital loopback (TX→RX in the PL). This is the PHY datapath over silicon, not yet an over-the-air / RF-front-end measurement.
