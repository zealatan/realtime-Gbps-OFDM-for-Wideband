ISS-19highopenintegration
ssc_slot_valid never fires in wrapper TB sim
Was misdiagnosed initially. Actual root cause: meyr_slot_frontend.slot_start was wired to slot_start_pulse (= rising edge of slot_start_controller.slot_valid, which fires AFTER frac_corrector's S_DONE = after the last corrected beat). By that cycle frac_m_tvalid had dropped, so meyr_slot_frontend's `if (slot_start && s_tvalid)` activation condition could never be true.
FIXED 2026-06-17 night by adding `frac_m_first_beat` signal (rising edge of frac_m_tvalid after frac_start_r) and re-wiring `u_slot_fe.slot_start` to it. ssc-derived slot_start_pulse is preserved for dbg_slot_start_valid downstream consumers.
